Building & Plot Approval Services

We manage municipal and Panchayat approvals from plan review through submission and authority follow-up. For eligible residential and industrial buildings, we also assess self-certification routes and help keep construction aligned with approved plans.

Building plan approval applications for municipalities and Panchayats
Bylaw review covering FSI/FAR, height, setbacks, plot coverage, and open space
Online single-window and offline plan submissions, including AutoDCR where applicable
Verification of title deeds, ownership records, patta or mutation, and encumbrance records
Layout and plot subdivision approvals through regional planning authorities
Self-certification eligibility checks for residential and industrial buildings
Pre-submission audits to align plans with applicable building bylaws
Digital submission of architectural plans, structural safety declarations, and owner affidavits
Application tracking, official acknowledgements, and clearance documentation
Post-submission checks that construction follows approved or self-certified drawings

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Eligibility & Document ReviewWe check the project category, land records, and approval route.
  2. Plan & Bylaw AuditDrawings are reviewed against applicable planning requirements.
  3. Submission & Follow-upWe prepare the application and coordinate with the relevant authority.
  4. Clearance & ComplianceWe share approval records and help track construction against approved plans.

Property Valuation & Estimation Services

Before selling, buying, gifting, inheriting, or taking legal action involving your property, you need an accurate, grounded valuation. We visit the site and compare real market transactions in the area to provide a professional assessment—not a guess.

Before selling — set the right asking price
Before buying — confirm a fair price
Inheritance and succession planning
Gifting between family members
Legal disputes and court submissions
Bank loans or mortgages against property
Capital gains calculation for tax planning

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Purpose & Property BriefWe understand the property details and why you need a valuation.
  2. Site VisitOur team visits the property to assess its condition, access, and features.
  3. Market ComparisonWe compare relevant local transactions and property factors.
  4. Valuation ReportYou receive a clear assessment to support your next decision.

Government Liaison Services

We leverage 35 years of deep-rooted regulatory expertise to act as your strategic bridge to government authorities. We don’t just submit paperwork; we understand the inner workings of municipal planning boards, environmental agencies, and utility departments. Our team manages the entire statutory clearance lifecycle, ensuring your project remains 100% compliant, fully authorized, and cleared for rapid groundbreaking.

Patta transfer and mutation at Taluk office
Chitta and Adangal — land records and cultivation updates
Encumbrance Certificate (EC) from Sub-Registrar Office
FMB sketch and survey number verification
Property tax name transfer and on-time payment
Certified copy of the document
Stability certificate

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Document CollectionWe collect all necessary documents from you.
  2. Verification & ReviewOur team verifies documents and details.
  3. Submission & Follow-upWe submit and follow up with departments.
  4. Completion & DeliveryYou receive the approved documents.
