Government Liaison Services

Navigating Tamil Nadu's revenue, registration, and local body offices from a distance is slow, stressful, and often impossible. Our team has established relationships across the region — attending on your behalf, handling queues, coordinating filings, keeping you updated at every step.

Patta transfer and mutation at Taluk office
Chitta and Adangal — land records and cultivation updates
Encumbrance Certificate (EC) from Sub-Registrar Office
FMB sketch and survey number verification
Property tax name transfer and on-time payment
Sale deed and registration facilitation
Stamp duty calculation and payment management
Building approval — DTCP and CMDA co-ordination
Power of Attorney drafting and attestation support
Court petition follow-up and legal representation

DOCUMENTS WE HANDLE

  • Patta / Chitta / Adangal
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
  • Sale Deed and Gift Deed registration
  • FMB Sketch and Survey Records
  • Building Plan Approval (DTCP / CMDA)
  • No-Objection Certificates (NOC)
  • Power of Attorney (PoA)
  • Succession Certificate assistance

OFFICES WE ATTEND

  • Taluk / Revenue Office
  • Sub-Registrar Office (SRO)
  • District Collector's Office
  • DTCP — Town and Country Planning
  • Local Body / Panchayat offices
  • Tamil Nadu Electricity Board (TNEB)
  • District Civil Courts

Property Valuation Services

Before selling, buying, gifting, inheriting, or taking any legal action involving your property — you need an accurate, grounded valuation. Not a guess, but a professional assessment conducted after physically visiting the site and comparing real market transactions in that area.

Physical site visit by our valuation expert
Comparable market transaction analysis — recent sales
Government guideline value (circle rate) assessment
Land dimensions, shape, and access road evaluation
Soil type, water availability, crop suitability for farmland
Legal encumbrance check — clear title confirmation
Development potential — DTCP approval, layout scope
Detailed written report with supporting photographs
Final market value range — for negotiation reference

WHEN YOU NEED A VALUATION

  • Before selling — set the right asking price
  • Before buying — confirm a fair price
  • Inheritance and succession planning
  • Gifting between family members
  • Legal disputes — court submission
  • Bank loan or mortgage against property
  • Capital gains calculation for tax planning

VALUATION FACTORS

  • Location and proximity to town / highway
  • Road frontage and access quality
  • Patta type — wet, dry, house site patta
  • Legal status — clear title vs pending dispute
  • Utilities — water, EB, drainage
  • Development potential and future growth
  • Crop standing value for farm land
Typical time5 - 7 Working DaysIdeal forSellers, Buyers, Legal Use

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Booking & BriefYou share property details and the purpose of valuation.
  2. Site VisitOur expert inspects the property in person.
  3. Market & Legal CheckComparable sales and title records are reviewed.
  4. Report DeliveryYou receive a written valuation report.
